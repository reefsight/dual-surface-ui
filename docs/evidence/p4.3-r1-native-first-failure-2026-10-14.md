`GetCurrentPropertyValue(..., ignoreDefaultValue: true)` and
`FailureDiagnostics.ReadRequired<T>` distinguishes the exact NotSupported
sentinel from malformed properties. Guard/root/fence/member paths require
typed `NativeWindowHandle` observations. The actual negative therefore
establishes an unsupported window-handle read during initial setup. It does
not identify the peer, its type, root/member role, physical HWND absence or
which specific caller failed. No new native getter was used to locate it.

and [GetCurrentPropertyValue documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.windows.automation.automationelement.getcurrentpropertyvalue?view=windowsdesktop-10.0)
distinguish a supplied value, NotSupported when defaults are ignored, and a
default zero. Those APIs do not establish this peer's ownership; substituting
zero or trusting missing metadata is not an accepted fix.
