## Accepted planning boundaries

The design covers seven pattern families without inventing a Selection mutation.
Radio uses its fixed current two-member proof, not a fabricated Selection
container. Group has current-state-only expand/collapse; Combo open/close stays
trusted setup, not an executable action or dual fixture-state oracle. Range uses
explicit current bounds and exact postconditions, without step/grid inference.
Provider blocking, popup retirement and possible effects cannot authorize retries.

Historical disclosure needs current terminal-revision equality and fresh host
authority. The supervisor reserves a bounded attempt before dispatching a
mutation-capable worker; worker loss retires the old session and retains
uncertainty. These are proposed implementation requirements, not measured native
behavior, authenticated IPC, durable recovery or full S4 lifecycle proof.

The separately discovered exported verifier defect has its own
[P4.1 maintenance brief](../work-items/native-protocol-replay-revision-remediation.md)
and source reviews. Its correction and fresh gates must be accepted separately
before later S3 entry; neither this decision nor a pure test supplies that
source/checkpoint acceptance or any native action evidence.
