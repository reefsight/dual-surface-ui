## Required review scope

### Protocol

- Confirm the prose, strict JSON Schemas, TypeScript projection, and frozen
  fixtures agree on every message, capability, limit, error, and transition.
- Confirm exact 0.x negotiation, immutable capabilities, reconnect invalidation,
  revision correlation, cancellation, replay, and event sequencing fail closed.
- Inspect the request/event race fix in the reviewed commit and verify that a
  late response cannot resurrect a closed surface or roll back a newer revision.

### Security

- Treat all client messages, model output, application content, protocol bytes,
  action input, and events as untrusted.
- Confirm callers cannot assert principal, OS user/session, process/window,
  policy, risk, confirmation, preconditions, expected effects, or raw handles.
- Confirm credentials, sensitive values, exceptions, stacks, platform errors,
  secret sentinels, and signing material do not cross fixture/error boundaries.
- Confirm this verifier is not represented as authentication, authorization,
  transport security, OS permission proof, or action execution.

### Interoperability

- Confirm all values are finite interoperable JSON, identifiers and strings are
  bounded, exact integers do not exceed the portable safe range, and source key
  order/whitespace are irrelevant outside explicitly canonical digest inputs.
- Confirm both corpora and their JSON Schemas contain no TypeScript, Rust, .NET,
  Swift, ABI, pointer, native-handle, or serializer-specific assumption.
- Confirm second-language execution is correctly deferred to the later language
  decision gate and is not falsely claimed as completed P4.1 evidence.

### Package

- Confirm `dual-surface-ui/native-protocol` is additive and the frozen root
  remains 32 exports, 6 declaration files, and 5 core schemas.
- Confirm the npm tarball includes only reviewed runtime/declarations, schemas,
  and fixture corpora, and contains no Cargo files, native binaries, experiment
  directory, credentials, or production data.

## Author precheck findings

The author-side review found one response/event ordering issue: a catalog refresh
or surface event could overtake an in-flight response. Commit
`033738e3c265d5b615630b6f9bee78c7fc897806` fixes it by binding each stateful
request to its request-time revision and requiring resynchronization on a third
revision or catalog replacement with unresolved stateful work. Two focused tests
and three portable transcripts cover the correction. No known critical/high
finding remains in the author precheck.

Known boundaries are intentional: P4.1 supplies no native host, OS adapter,
transport, peer authentication, authorization engine, daemon, signing,
installer, distribution, or real-OS proof. Rust remains preferred but is not
selected before the roadmap's comparative decision gate.
