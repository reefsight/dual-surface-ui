## P44-MD-SEC-D01 - Medium: row clocks and derived values need an explicit trusted binding

References: measurement brief lines 89-108, 149-161 and 187-205.

The design correctly distinguishes runner end-to-end clocks from candidate
internal phases, freezes clock profiles, uses bounded decimal strings and
requires positive frequency. However, its sample-row grammar admits a row's
`clockRef`, `frequency` and `value` without expressly requiring an exact match
to the trusted immutable clock profile for that metric/process lifetime, or
recomputing derived values rather than accepting row summaries. "Same named
clock" and generic interval semantics leave this material cross-field
admissibility condition implicit.

Static counterexample, not an executed sample or native exploit:

1. A trusted profile uses 10,000,000 ticks/second for a runner interval.
2. A row correctly reports a 10,000,000-tick elapsed interval and a plausible
   planned slot/case/clock label, but reports frequency 1,000,000,000,000.
3. That row still has unsigned bounded decimal fields, positive frequency and
   ordered endpoints. The stated formula yields 0.01 ms instead of 1,000 ms.
4. A digest of those bytes and complete planned counts do not establish that
   its denominator is the admitted clock's actual frequency. A candidate phase
   could similarly reuse a label across process restart or supply a derived
   value inconsistent with its endpoints.

The document promises trusted attribution rather than candidate-reported
numbers; the validator must explicitly enforce that promise. The future closed
table schema alone is not a substitute for this invariant. This is an omission
in the proposed design, not a claim that an existing validator accepts it.

Required design refinement:

- Bind each timing metric/phase to an exact reviewed clock role, clock profile
  and opaque process/clock-instance lifetime. Resolve those from the admitted
  schedule and owned instrumentation, never from candidate-selected row fields.
- Require exact equality of row frequency to that immutable trusted profile,
  the same correlated origin/lifetime for both endpoints, and fixed units and
  resolution. Missing, mismatched or mixed origins/instances fail admission;
  an owned process reference alone is not clock provenance.
- Derive duration, CPU ratios and throughput from admitted raw endpoints,
  counters and independently admitted verified counts using bounded checked
  rational arithmetic. Do not let a supplied `value`, display rounding or a
  renamed clock override authoritative operands. Any permitted redundant
  summary must exactly agree or be refused.
- Define positive denominators for ratio metrics; a zero wall interval or
  insufficient counter resolution cannot become favorable zero, infinity or
  a qualifying ratio. Preserve the descriptive/resolution limitation instead.
- Require future negative vectors for frequency substitution, wrong clock role,
  process restart/lifetime reuse, mixed endpoints, wrong raw unit, derived-value
  mismatch, zero denominators, malformed/oversized integers and overflow/lossy
  conversion boundaries. Keep the original raw bytes and refusal visible.

Root was notified before this report. No clock API, candidate instrumentation
or calculation implementation is authorized or accepted by this recommendation.

## P44-MD-SEC-D02 - Medium: candidate-child counter membership needs bounded creation authority

References: measurement brief lines 58-86, 112-129 and 209-223; accepted
native/security strategy and P4.5 owned-process/isolation requirements.

CPU and memory are defined over the candidate and "every admitted owned
candidate child", with exited-child acquisition frozen later. The method does
not explicitly define the bounded complete process set, who may create/admit
children, or how lifetime identity is retained. This leaves room for an
apparently valid metric to omit work performed by an unadmitted child, or for
counter collection to acquire a process from a candidate-reported PID or
recycled process identifier. Neither a name nor ancestry alone establishes
trusted ownership, and missing membership is not necessarily detectable as
"missing child data" if the collector never had an admitted complete set.

Static counterexamples, not observed process behavior:

- A candidate performs expensive work in a helper not represented in its
  admitted set; parent-only CPU/working-set data look complete and artificially
  favorable unless unexpected children are themselves a qualification refusal.
- A child exits during the observation window before a final CPU sample. If
  only live members are totaled, its consumed CPU disappears. Treating its
  unavailable counter as zero misclassifies incomplete coverage as a result.
- A retained PID is reused or a candidate supplies a claimed child identifier.
  Looking it up or recursively enumerating processes to repair membership can
  read/charge another process and broaden the original owned-process scope.

The design's general prohibition on raw identities in output is sound, but
does not by itself prevent an internal counter-acquisition authority error.
Future source review is required; this design should fix the ownership invariant
before letting that source choose a process-acquisition method.

Required design refinement:

- Freeze a complete bounded expected candidate-process topology: roles, maximum
  live and total created counts, depth, allowed lifetimes and planned creation/
  exit accounting. The fixture/runner/observer roles stay separately attributed.
- Obtain counter authority only from trusted runner-owned creation/admission
  capabilities, exact retained handles plus birth/lifetime identity, or an
  explicitly reviewed bounded owned-container mechanism. A candidate PID,
  process name, first matching descendant or arbitrary process lookup cannot
  provide it. No global/user-process enumeration is a membership repair path.
- Deny unexpected/unadmitted child creation or classify the candidate/run as
  unqualified/incomplete; do not silently omit it from the denominator or add
  a new process authority after work has been performed. Refuse process-count/
  depth/resource limits before unbounded growth or counter collection.
- Preserve initial/final CPU data for every admitted lifetime, including exited
  children, over the exact common observation window. Missing required final
  accounting remains incomplete, not zero. Resident samples must use that same
  admitted set and disclose lifetime/cadence coverage; they remain sampled sums,
  not an atomic physical-memory peak.
- Public/private retained evidence uses opaque role/lifetime references only,
  not raw PID/handle/user/path identity. Freeze limits and accounting before
  results, with lifetime reuse, unexpected/missing child, exit-before-final-
  counter, duplicate attribution and count/depth negatives in later pure/source
  and separately authorized real proof.

  including policy/confirmation/identity/revision verification, failures,
  concurrency/cancellation/event-loss and resource qualification. A favorable
  microbenchmark or language preference cannot offset unauthorized effects,
  leakage, permission or delivery failures. Exclusions/failures remain visible.
- Runner endpoints include admission and authoritative validation; mutation-call
  timing is separately candidate-internal and not a business success or permission
  result. Events/resync need trusted trigger/injection ledgers and fresh state;
  callback counts/timestamps cannot prove notification completeness.
- Expected negatives are separate qualified semantic cases under frozen refusal/
  effect criteria. Unknown effects, unexpected timeout/crash/guard/permission
  refusal and incomplete required metrics do not become successful latencies,
  favorable zero or routine retries. Shared-host/fixture safety uncertainty stops
  the campaign for separately reviewed continuation. Repetition counts are not
  permission to rerun an unexpected failed attempt unchanged.
- Medians/p95 are homogeneous exact rational series, not cross-case pooling or
  rounded ranking. No trimming/replacement/post-result warmup is allowed; partial
  descriptive data are labeled incomplete and cannot qualify or select a runtime.
- CPU, resident sampled sums, private memory, throughput, packaged/installed size
  and dependency/build evidence remain distinct metrics. Sampled working-set
  peak is not unique physical RAM or an unsampled absolute peak. Security/update/
  contribution qualification is not reduced to a weighted performance score.
- Hashes bind bytes, not producer truth. Independent source/owned-origin and
  original-byte artifact admission remain separate obligations. A source digest,
  manifest field or candidate-reported result is not provenance or authority.

explicitly deferred to exact reviewed source/manifest freezes, not arbitrary

4 KiB per row; 128 UTF-8-byte labels; 1,024 listed shards; 256 MiB metadata total.

parser must enforce strict UTF-8/decoded duplicate keys/closed shapes and checked
counts before allocation; exact parser depth/token/collection/arithmetic/work
bounds and streaming behavior still need actual adversarial source review.
I have not proved that the full future workload and original artifact inventory
fit these ceilings. The design itself requires that pre-result fit check or a
separately reviewed larger method, never selective case omission.

Logical file IDs/digests resolve only within an admitted owned campaign manifest.
No traversal, external path/URL, linked file, arbitrary directory enumeration
or current D2 partial artifact is authority. Exact opened-file size/hash/path/
identity controls and original bytes remain mandatory; sequential checks/hashes
are not an atomic defense against a privileged filesystem editor. Future source
must implement, test and honestly limit those controls rather than assuming the
prose proves them.

The privacy classification fits controlled synthetic evidence. Safe coarse host/

### SEC-D01 resolved in the design

The final timing section expressly binds each metric/phase to its trusted
instrumentation role, opaque process epoch and clock-instance profile through
the admitted schedule. Candidate/provider declarations cannot create or select
clock authority. Acquisition/units/frequency/resolution rules are frozen before
data; trusted birth records bind later actual lifetimes to the plan without
claiming future native identities were known in advance.

Both endpoints must share that instance. Row frequency must exactly equal the
immutable admitted profile; exited-clock origins cannot be reused. Timing rows
require null `value`, while any future redundant derived summary needs reviewed
exact representation and agreement with independently recomputed operands.
Durations, CPU ratios and throughput derive from admitted raw endpoints,
lifetime counters, wall windows and independently verified cycle counts.
Zero denominators or insufficient resolution make required ratios incomplete/
refused, not a favorable value. Exact bounded rational arithmetic and display-
only rounding remain mandatory.

This explicitly rejects the initial frequency-substitution and lifetime/derived-
value counterexamples. The final row admission also names clock-profile equality
and topology/lifetime membership; the required later vectors include frequency/
role/origin/epoch/unit/value substitution, mixed endpoints, zero denominator,
insufficient resolution and arithmetic overflow/oversize. These are reviewable
requirements, not executed calculation or instrumentation results.

### SEC-D02 resolved in the design

The final topology section freezes roles, parent relations, window membership,
depth, live/total lifetimes and planned birth/exit slots. Proposed upper ceilings
are 32 live owned candidate processes, depth 4 and 16,384 lifetimes per candidate
campaign; the exact admitted finite schedule may lower but not silently enlarge
them. Enlarging a method requires review before data, not helper omission.

Counter authority now requires runner-created retained creation/lifetime
capabilities or a separately reviewed owned containment mechanism. Candidate
PID/name assertions, relookup after exit, broad enumeration and first-match
descendant discovery are expressly forbidden. Unexpected/missing/escaping
children, reused lifetimes, duplicate attribution and absent exit data refuse
or invalidate the observation rather than disappearing from cost. Detection/
containment and pre-admission count/depth enforcement must be frozen before runs.

Each admitted CPU lifetime needs initial and final counters, including exited
children and generations replaced by planned recovery. Raw units are fixed,
converted and exactly summed with membership/coverage records; missing final
data are not zero. Resident samples use the same admitted set and record cadence,
sampling-window width and exit coverage. The sampled peak is the maximum of
same-cadence sums, not the sum of independent process peaks; sequential reads
are explicitly non-atomic and gaps follow a frozen refusal tolerance.

The future negatives include reuse, unplanned/missing/escaping child, exit before
final accounting, duplicate charge and live/total/depth overflow. This resolves
the proposed ownership/accounting ambiguity while retaining the crucial limit:
the prose does not implement or prove OS containment, counter acquisition,
resource enforcement, atomic sampling or exited-process data availability.

### Other revised safety and evidence boundaries reviewed

I independently assessed the additional revisions without claiming their
initial discovery or another role's execution evidence:

- A whole-cycle runner timer spans first dispatch through final admitted
  verification continuously. Between-case reset/setup, waits/delays and planned
  restart/reinitialization remain timed; active-case sums cannot replace it.
  The denominator explicitly excludes/discloses before-cycle preparation, so
  complete-cycle throughput is not misrepresented as whole-campaign throughput.
- Warm denotes a campaign phase, not universal hot process reuse. Fatal/restart
  cases retain fresh epochs and their initialization costs inside the cycle;
  cohorts are frozen before results. A planned crash/recovery interval requires
  old-process exit and verified fresh generation/session/catalog/capture plus
  old-reference/uncertainty checks. A restart alone is not recovery proof, and
  a terminal fault contract cannot become a recovered latency. No P4.5 durable
  host/authentication implementation is claimed.
- The manifest/rows explicitly add topology, epoch, counts and terminal-stop
  bindings. Each planned slot is a metric observation, not automatically a
  complete case/cycle. Expected negative cases are valid only under independently
  verified frozen refusal/terminal semantics, not mislabeled unexpected failures.
- Complete qualifying packets require every slot, required original artifact/
  verification references and null stop. Stopped negative admission instead
  requires an admitted contiguous original prefix plus a separately trusted
  terminal envelope for the next attempted slot. Missing terminal rows remain
  absent; optional failure rows cannot replace the stop record or carry passing
  verification. No gaps/duplicates/post-stop successes or contradictory counts
  are admitted, and negative/partial packets cannot qualify or rank candidates.
- Final count equations explicitly distinguish packet disposition from metric
  slots: complete attempted = valid = planned, with other slot counts zero;
  a normal stopped prefix has one failed/invalid/aborted attempted terminal slot.
  Pre-dispatch abort has zero attempted slots, while post-run packet invalidation
  may retain all valid slots without inventing a failed slot or qualifying the
  candidate. Root self-caught the earlier ambiguous "all other counts" wording;

- Malformed original bytes stay quarantined. If even the prefix cannot be
  admitted, a bound negative terminal envelope may be preserved but dataset
  admission is rejected; rows/counts are not repaired or reserialized into
  original evidence. Hashes still bind bytes, not native truth or authority.
