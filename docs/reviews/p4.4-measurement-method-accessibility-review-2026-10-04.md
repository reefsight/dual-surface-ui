## Finding MD-AI01 - complete-cycle throughput boundary

Severity: Medium. Status: unresolved in the initial reviewed target.

The method's lines 74-81 require 100 warm complete ordered workload cycles, but
exclude fixture setup/reset and untimed warm preparation from reported intervals.
Lines 130-133 define throughput as complete verified cycles divided by their
runner elapsed seconds. A complete workload has between-case setup/reset and
may contain required lifecycle/restart cases. The proposal does not specify
whether those costs are inside the cycle denominator.

A continuous first-dispatch-to-final-verification timer includes intervening
resets, delays and restart costs. Summing case-only intervals, pausing the timer,
or preparing replacement workers outside that timer produces a different metric.
Both readings fit the current text and could favor candidates differently despite
identical case counts. Calling either result complete-cycle end-to-end throughput
would conceal the difference.

Required resolution before approving this method: freeze the exact cycle start
and end; distinguish untimed before-cycle preparation from unavoidable
between-case reset/setup/wait/restart work; explicitly prohibit stopping or
restarting the whole-cycle clock to exclude that work. If an active-time sum is
useful, name and report it separately. Record fresh candidate-process epochs in
fatal/restart cases so 100 warm cycles cannot hide fresh-worker initialization as
untimed preparation or imply all observations share one warm process lifetime.

I sent this finding to the producer before report publication. The producer
acknowledged it and described an intended revision. That message is not a reviewed
correction; only a separately bound full re-review can close this finding.

## Other assessed requirements

The following are adequate at this design stage; they are not implemented or
measured properties:

- Thirty fresh candidate-process starts are distinguished from OS reboot or cold
  filesystem cache. Cold-start latency includes candidate admission, transport and
  the first verified capture of an already prepared fixture, not fixture startup.
- Runner-side capture/action/event/resync endpoints include validation and
  authoritative refreshed state. Provider-call time is expressly a separate
  internal series, not effect latency. Action timing includes current policy,
  confirmation, identity/revision and verification; both fresh native and
  independent fixture-state postconditions remain required.
- Same-clock monotonic endpoints, raw ticks/frequency, explicit clock profiles
  and rejection of cross-clock subtraction preserve timer meaning. UTC is not an
  elapsed-time origin. Descriptive median/nearest-rank p95 examples are internally
  consistent; no executable arithmetic validator was run by this reviewer.
- Trusted trigger/injection ledgers and refreshed state, not provider timestamps
  or a local callback counter alone, define event/resync observations. Future
  source review must still prove admitted native origin and distinguish known
  injected loss from provider coalescing or unobserved notifications; refreshed
  state alone is not proof of every notification's delivery.
- CPU includes admitted owned candidate children, while fixture/runner/observer
  costs are separately disclosed. Memory is explicitly a sampled summed working
  set, not private commit, unique physical RAM or an unsampled absolute peak. The
  instrumentation freeze should make the peak the maximum over simultaneous
  summed samples, not a sum of independent process peaks, and retain exited-child
  CPU accounting and sampling coverage.
- Equal Windows workload, accepted fixture semantics, fixed case order/reset
  seeds, concurrency, deadlines and injection schedules apply to all viable
  candidates. Counterbalancing and cooldown are frozen before results. The
  later macOS assessment cannot substitute for Windows workload qualification.
- Required negatives have their own dispositions; unexpected failure stops the
  affected run, retains original evidence and withholds qualification. Missing
  metrics, unsupported required cases, timeouts and incomplete series cannot
  become zero-valued successes or silently disappear from ranking.
- Closed bounded original-byte evidence admission, fixed logical references,
  redacted metadata and separate origin/ownership proof are appropriate design
  requirements. Hashes and a valid manifest alone are expressly not native proof.
- Qualification precedes ranking. P4.5 package/isolation/update credibility is a
  candidate design prerequisite, not circular acceptance of an unselected P4.5
  implementation. Original review and explicit maintainer-choice gates remain.

### MD-AI01 resolution and whole-method assessment

MD-AI01 is resolved in the final target. Lines 79-92 fix the whole-cycle start
immediately before first dispatch and end after last required verification,
include every intervening reset/setup/queue/wait/delay/restart initialization,
and prohibit pausing, restarting or substituting an active-interval sum. The
warm phase retains hot/fresh process cohorts and initialization costs for planned
fatal/restart cases. Lines 204-213 define the denominator from those complete
continuous intervals, disclose excluded before-cycle preparation and distinguish
separately frozen campaign throughput, which includes between-cycle preparation.
Thus the original two incompatible denominator interpretations no longer fit.

The full revision also preserves or strengthens the other assessed boundaries:

- Clock role, process epoch and clock instance are admitted rather than chosen
  by candidate labels. Both endpoints and redundant frequency must match the
  immutable profile; derived durations/ratios are independently recomputed.
  Cross-clock/UTC subtraction, zero denominators and insufficient resolution
  cannot yield qualifying values.
- Provider-call and verification phases remain distinct from end-to-end action
  latency. Current policy, confirmation, identity/revision and both fresh native
  and independent fixture postconditions remain in the required action scope.
  Trusted trigger/injection origin and authoritative refresh, not a local event
  counter's assumed completeness, still govern event/resync observations.
- Planned crash/recovery is a fixed workload case with old-process exit,
  new generation/session/catalog, first correct capture and required stale-binding
  refusal/uncertainty evidence. Restart alone is not recovery proof; accepted
  terminal faults and unexpected crashes are not quietly successful recoveries.
- Bounded owned topology, retained lifetime capabilities, exit-counter coverage
  and rejection of unplanned/missing/escaping/reused children prevent selectively
  omitting candidate helpers. Sampled memory peak is explicitly the maximum of
  per-cadence sums, not independent process peaks; sequential-read width and
  lifetime coverage are disclosed rather than called atomic or absolute peak.
- Complete metric-slot admission, expected negative verification, stopped valid
  prefixes, absent terminal rows, pre-dispatch abort and post-run invalidation
  have distinct reconciled rules. Packet-level failure does not fabricate a
  failed slot, successful terminal verification or repaired original row.
  Malformed originals stay quarantined; incomplete/negative packets cannot rank
  a candidate. The count equations are consistent with these distinctions.
- Thirty fresh starts and 100 complete case-preserving warm cycles, common
  Windows host/workload/seeds/order/concurrency/budgets, counterbalancing,
  overhead disclosure and no result-driven retry/outlier removal remain intact.
  A later macOS assessment is separate, not substitute Windows qualification.
- Original P4.4 AC-01 through AC-18 and ADR decision rules remain unchanged.
  Qualification precedes ranking; no speed score offsets safety or delivery
  failure, and P4.5 credibility means candidate design evidence, not circular
  acceptance of an unselected runtime's P4.5 implementation.
