## Accepted planning boundaries

The method keeps at least 30 fresh candidate-process starts and 100 complete
ordered warm workloads, including required accepted P4.3 cases, without pooling
different cases or dropping difficult ones. Its continuous cycle timer includes
all intervening reset/setup/wait/restart initialization; before-cycle preparation
is disclosed, not confused with whole-campaign throughput. Planned crash/recovery
needs observed exit, fresh admitted generation/state and required stale-reference
refusal and uncertainty/reconciliation, not merely a replacement process.

Trusted schedule/role/process-epoch/clock-instance profiles supply immutable
frequency and endpoint authority; raw operands, not candidate summaries, supply
derived values. Bounded topology and retained lifetime authority account for
owned children, including exited generations. CPU, sampled resident sums, private
memory and delivery/security effort remain distinct. None of these requirements
is implemented clock, containment, sampler or native recovery proof.

Complete qualifying packets require every scheduled verified slot. Stopped
negative prefixes, absent terminal rows, pre-dispatch aborts, post-run invalidation
and quarantined malformed originals have separate integrity/count rules and
cannot qualify or rank a candidate. Private bounded original-byte admission,
no identity/secret disclosure, homogeneous rational median/nearest-rank p95 and
qualification before ranking remain mandatory. Future exact closed shapes,
source/counter mechanisms, checked arithmetic, adversarial tests, complete-workload
fit and independently observed overhead still need their own source and entry.

Source implementation is distinct from measurement execution; calculation and
negative tests must pass before actual measurement, not before their own
separately authorized implementation. No source entry follows from this decision.
Credible candidate host/isolation/sign/update designs are P4.4 qualification,
not circular prior acceptance or claimed implementation of an unselected P4.5 host.
The original conditional Rust rule, explicit later strategy decision and unchanged
TypeScript/protocol/web boundary remain intact.
