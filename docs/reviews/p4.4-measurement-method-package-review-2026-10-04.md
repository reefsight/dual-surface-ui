### MD-P01 — Medium: required crash/recovery measurement has no defined series

P4.4 explicitly requires recording crash/recovery behavior in its frozen method
(`P4.4-native-language-decision.md:57`) and AC-08 (`:104`). The new timing table
(`P4.4-measurement-method-design.md:99` through `:105`) defines cold start,
capture/action, mutation/verification, event and resync, but not crash/recovery.
Cold start measures a new process against an already prepared fixture; resync
starts with gap/reorder/loss injection. Neither defines the expected candidate
crash, process/provider-loss boundary, retained uncertainty, replacement session
or recovery endpoint. The later unexpected-crash stop rule (`:165` through
`:170`) does not supply a planned crash/recovery metric or completeness test.

This permits a future metric inventory to omit an original mandatory requirement
or silently substitute cold-start/resync latency while still claiming complete
qualification. Define the mandatory expected crash/recovery case family and
same-clock trigger/end authority, observed exit and recovery/uncertainty fields,
units, counts and completion/refusal rules. Distinguish a planned fault case
from an unexpected crash that stops the run. Recovery must require fresh admitted
generation/state and the accepted uncertainty semantics, not merely a restarted
process or reuse of stale references. Preserve the later P4.5 implementation gate;
candidate behavior/design evidence cannot claim production host acceptance.

### MD-P02 — Medium: complete-success and stopped-failure admission are conflated

The method stops at first unexpected failure and retains censored/incomplete
observations (`:165` through `:178`), while sample admission requires expected
counts and no gaps without a disposition-dependent rule (`:197` through `:206`).
The manifest includes `disposition`, but does not define how that changes the
count/order/completeness predicate. A legitimate stopped prefix cannot contain
the full planned sample count; a worker may also stop before publishing a row.
An implementation could therefore discard the failure as structurally invalid,
or weaken full-count admission generally and accept missing success samples.

Specify separate closed admission branches. A complete qualifying campaign must
contain every planned slot and all required verified artifacts. A failed/invalid/
aborted campaign may retain only a source-attributed contiguous attempted prefix
plus an exact terminal failure/abort record and coherent planned, attempted,
valid, failed and unattempted counts. A missing terminal sample must remain absent,
not become a synthetic original row. Reject success after the terminal stop,
duplicates, interior gaps, count contradictions and passing verification on a
failed row. Admit such records as negative evidence only; never allow them to
supply qualifying aggregates or candidate ranking. Require future adversarial
vectors for both branches before any measurement entry.

## Independently checked positive boundaries

The proposal preserves at least 30 fresh candidate-process starts and 100
complete ordered warm workloads per candidate. It explicitly rejects counting
one easy case as a full cycle and retains separate homogeneous case-level
series. The future manifest must freeze the schedule, host/profile, resets,
seeds, workload, case mix, concurrency, budgets, instrumentation and overhead
before results. Process-cold is correctly distinguished from OS/cache-cold.

Timing starts and ends use one named monotonic clock per series. Runner
end-to-end measurements include admission/transport/validation; candidate-local
mutation and verification phases cannot be silently summed across unrelated
clock origins or substituted for business-result latency. UTC is not duration
authority. Qualification cases with expected refusal/uncertainty remain distinct
from successful-action timings. The actual instrumentation and overhead have
not been implemented or observed by this reviewer.

The mathematical definitions are coherent by direct hand checking: nearest-rank
p95 for 1..30 selects ceil(28.5)=29, median is (15+16)/2=15.5; for 1..100 the
corresponding values are 95 and 50.5. For [1,1,2,2,9], they are 9 and 2.
Exact rational tick/frequency ordering precedes display rounding. Timeout values
are censored failures, not fabricated successful durations; incomplete or
unsupported required metrics cannot qualify or rank. No calculation program
or unit test was executed during this prose review.

CPU includes candidate and admitted candidate-child user+kernel time, with
exited-child accounting required and fixture/runner/observer CPU separate.
The wall denominator and multi-core percentages are explicit. Memory is summed
resident working-set bytes with shared-page double counting and sampled-peak
limitations disclosed; it is not falsely called unique/private/absolute-peak
memory. Throughput counts complete verified workloads with a fixed case mix,
not messages or mutation calls. Trusted trigger/injection ledgers and fresh
state, not callback counters, ground event metrics. Artifact/archive/install
sizes and dependency/cache assumptions remain separate.

The proposed JSON/JSONL evidence is private, bounded and not a new public
protocol schema. Byte/row/label/shard/total ceilings are cumulative requirements;
the future full workload must fit without filtering cases. Logical artifact IDs,
exact original-byte digests, regular owned-file/path checks and refusal of
duplicates/traversal/external references preserve the existing privacy/package
boundary. Hashes are not truth or native-origin proof. Nested shapes, enums,
metric cross-fields, arithmetic and readers still need an exact future freeze,
source review and negative tests; this review does not admit an implemented
manifest, parser, runner or measurement artifact.

Safety qualification precedes ranking and cannot be offset by speed or memory.
The unchanged Rust rule and explicit later maintainer decision prevent a
synthetic win or preference from selecting a language. Requiring credible
candidate authentication/isolation/sign/update designs, rather than accepted
P4.5 implementation, correctly avoids circular P4.4/P4.5 entry. It does not
waive real candidate semantic/security qualification or later host acceptance.
