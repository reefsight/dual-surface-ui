## Reviewed behavior and trust boundary

**Passive observation, not admission authority.** The final design permits only
private enum check/guard literals at the existing constructor/helper boundaries.
An existing explicit Unavailable guard notifies once and still throws its
original closed refusal unconditionally. The observer cannot change a Boolean
condition, short-circuiting, native call/read order, allocation bound, deadline
or successful outcome. Callback faults are withheld rather than replacing the
guard. Unexpected enum/context input yields only the original coarse refusal,
not a default, inferred safety value or partial pair. The callback itself is
restricted to closed pair/context validation and one immutable assignment:
no IO, logging, clock/deadline check or native/provider operation, even for
invalid or already-latched input.

**Constructor scope and first-primary lifecycle.** Observer capability exists
only during that actual construction and is cleared in its finally on success
or refusal. Subsequent Observe/context/peer checks and cleanup remain coarse.
The three allowed case/stage/site contexts match all existing suite constructor
calls, including window replacement and restart, without authorizing an extra
admission. The two optional fields are validated and committed together with
the immutable first tuple. Ordinary failure latches initialize both null;
later notification or cleanup cannot overwrite the pair. SDK/managed faults
or deadline refusal are not tagged merely because a check was last entered.
The fixed serial caller remains the bound assumption; immutable tuple assignment
is not a claim of native/process atomicity or arbitrary concurrent callback
safety. Once-only owned cleanup and one-shot publication remain required.

**Group meaning and safety defaults.** Static enumeration of the final table
finds12 checks,17 distinct guard literals and40 allowed pairs. Compound labels
identify source groups only, not the rejected Boolean operand or actual OS
value. The three token contexts retain the same eight guard groups and no SID
kind/value/data; desktop-name-or-null does not claim a name was observed.
No Name/content/default/pattern read, token/desktop re-query, PID/HWND selection,
global enumeration or after-refusal probe is permitted. Nothing in the new
pair can qualify a UIA target, substitute explicit typed safety, provide native
authority, relax stale-membership proof or add an eighth semantic pattern family.

**Exact separate negative wire contract.** The new distinct D2 kind has exactly
fourteen required string/null keys, including a both-null-or-valid-pair relation.
A non-null pair is legal only for guard-refused/Unavailable and one of the three
exact constructor contexts. Other stages/cases/sites/classes/codes, half-pairs,
extra keys or unknown literals refuse. The fourteen-field flat object consumes
30 JSON tokens including root delimiters, within the unchanged32-token cap;
4096 original bytes, depth2, decoded-string128, strict UTF8/BOM/UTF16/duplicate/
trailing-JSON checks need no widening. Existing digest, calendar/timestamp,
nullable cleanup and stage/site/class/code conditions remain mandatory.

The D1 twelve-key/kind parser stays a distinct exact historical negative-only
contract. A current production CLI admits only the new fixed D2 freeze and
explicit D2 path, not a guessed kind, D1 fallback or caller-selected pins. The
guarded single-file reader still opens only original failure bytes and emits
their digest with nativeAccepted:false; it never inventories or decodes partial
evidence content. The unchanged success topology rejects failure.json. Current
working-tree/binary changes cannot silently inherit earlier D1 admission proof.

**Recursive binding and decisions.** The design explicitly adds itself to the
component's11-path and suite's18-path manifests. Changed admission bytes require
a genuinely new component digest, independently reproduced and pinned in both
suite binders; staleaf7 cannot bind them. Foundation79, collector/projector/
topology, fixture/goldens, S1 and public/protocol/package boundaries remain
unchanged. New fixed D2 source reports/whole-report blobs, binary/fixture pins,
freeze and execution decision prevent authority reuse. The private managed-unit
summary ceiling may change256 to512 only if needed and tested; that conditional
pure reporting change does not widen stdout/stderr,10/30/240 budgets or native
22/32 coverage. Design, implementation entry, source review, prerequisite gates,
execution entry, actual artifacts and eventual S2 decision remain separate.
